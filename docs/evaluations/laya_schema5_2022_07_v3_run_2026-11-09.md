## Findings

1. **Fill-time rejection is the main loss of activity: 4 of 9 queued BUYs (44%) failed at the open.**
   - Controller sizing sets the exact §2a/§2b maximum at the prior close plus 5 bps. Any higher open then breaks §2a,
     and the fill check never resizes.
   - This is rule and controller design working as written, not an agent error. It was predicted from the smoke run
     and is now measured.
   - Smallest next step: decide the policy (size with a buffer, or quantity ≤ required) in a separate,
     explicitly authorized change.
2. **Hermes repeatedly picks stops at the edge of the 12% band.**
   - DB72 (twice) and TV77 were proposed with stops about 11.6–11.7% below entry, and failed §6a after small gap-ups.
   - This is agent judgment: AGENTS.md asks for margin at the 3% floor but says nothing about the ceiling.
3. **One self-report arithmetic error was caught by validation and retried.**
   - The `response_validation` ERROR: JG75's stated reward-to-risk was 1.52 against 0.51 from its own prices.
   - The retry produced a valid reply. JG75 was later queued and then vetoed by the pre-fill news gate.
   - This is an agent mistake contained by the controller. No sizing arithmetic is asked of Hermes any more, and
     none failed.
